### HOW IT ALL STARTED:

Funnily enough, Computer & Information Science wasn't my choice of study at all — but I must have been blinded by the light, because it has easily become one of my many favorite things. 

My obsession with coding actually begun back in my senior years of primary school through sheer necessity while playing a mobile game called **The Sims FreePlay**. Desperate for in-game currency, my inquisitive nature led me to hunt down a loophole — a duplication glitch that completely broke the game's economy in my favor. 

That childhood urge to look under the hood and manipulate how systems work is exactly what drives me today. Instead of exploiting mobile glitches, I now write the structured logic that makes the machines behave.
